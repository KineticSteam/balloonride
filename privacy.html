Balloon Ride Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Balloon Ride (the "Game"), developed by Kinetic Steam ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at kineticsteam001@gmail.com.

1. Short summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other information to play.
  • Your progress is stored on your device and, if enabled, in your device's Google backup.
  • The Game shows ads and offers in-app purchases. For this we use Google services, which may collect some device data as described below.
  • The Game is intended for people aged 13 and over.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, houses, armor, achievements, records) and your settings (sound, vibration, notifications) on your device. This information is not sent to any server of ours. Uninstalling the Game or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it from your device.

Backup: if Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may save a copy of your progress to your Google account (Google Drive) so it can be restored if you reinstall the Game or change devices. Google manages this copy under its privacy policy; we do not have access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

3. Information collected by third-party services

We use the following services, which may automatically collect information under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads): may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, language and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and your choice, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.
  • Google Play (purchases and downloads): purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment account details; we only receive the purchase confirmation so we can deliver your content.
  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reports): may collect anonymous usage data (for example, which screens are opened and how long a session lasts), technical device information and crash reports, to improve the Game.

Learn how Google uses this data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How information is used

  • To run the Game and save your progress.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To process in-app purchases.
  • To understand how the Game is used, fix bugs and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and cheating.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules notifications on your device (for example, "your house is repaired" or "your daily reward is waiting"). These are created on your device and do not send information to any server. You can turn them off in the Game's Settings or in your device settings.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: you can change your choice in the consent message (where applicable) or reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • Delete your Game data: use "Reset progress" in Settings or uninstall the Game.
  • Depending on the laws of your country (for example the GDPR in Europe, U.S. state privacy laws or Argentina's Law 25,326), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain uses. Since we do not keep personal data on our own servers, for third-party data you can use Google's tools, or contact us and we will help.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and security

Game data stays on your device while the Game is installed. Third-party services keep their data according to their own policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is 100% secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If there are important changes, we will let you know in the Game or on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
